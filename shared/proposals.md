# Custom corporate proposals

Custom corporate proposals are branded PDFs built from Google Drive folder Proposal Studio (id `1NT5slGj16hrxjRgMJndZ2h7We-0_78ur`) using `Proposal-Template.html`, locked to the ABBYY June 2026 layout.

## Defaults

- File name: `Company_Headshot_Proposal.pdf`
- Valid 30 days
- Turnaround: 2-3 business days
- One final image per person unless specified
- On-site needs: 8x10 ft plus an outlet
- Full usage rights, copyright retained
- Sample grid: 4 images from `Headshot photos/cropped/`
- No em dashes
- Follow-up drafts on day 3 and day 8 only if she says yes. Do not auto-draft them.
- Stamp SAMPLE on sample PDFs so they are not mixed up with real jobs

## Sample image folders inside Proposal Studio

- `Headshot photos/cropped` (about 12 cropped JPGs) for headshot PDFs
- `event photos` (about 15 JPGs, Dell luncheon style) for event PDFs
- `uatx sample images` (12 larger UATX shots)

Drop replacements in those same folders.

## Cover email (SC@): copy the Route shape

This is the working template. Tone: short, warm, direct. Hi FirstName. Best, Suzanne. First-touch photo signature. Typed close is Suzanne.

Length: three short paragraphs. No emojis. No em dashes.

Structure:
1. Thank + I do this on location (or the matching who / where / when / headcount fact).
2. PDF attached. Pricing lives in the PDF. Date flexibility if they gave a window.
3. Offer a call. Hold once date and venue are known.

Do not dump a price table in the email. Point at the PDF. Offer a specific next step (call + hold).

Start a clean subject next time, e.g. Leadership Headshots for Your Austin Offsite. Do not copy leftover form subjects.

Older one-sentence cover rule is retired. We now prefer this three-paragraph Route shape.
