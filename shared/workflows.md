# Client lifecycle

Qualify before quoting. Same-day acknowledge. Quote within 24 hours of qualification.

Do not merge old Cowork Claude scheduler times into Ingrid inbox rules. Inbox sweep times stay in [inbox-rules.md](inbox-rules.md).

## Inquiry-to-Booking

Qualify the job before a dollar number. Same-day acknowledge on the inbound thread. If the job is a group or on-site, ask Suzanne for the dollar number first. See [proposals.md](proposals.md) and [business.md](business.md).

Sprout New Inquiry drip (W1) auto-sends real email. Never backdate an inquiry date. If she already replied personally, warn before creating a lead. After a lead where drip should not run, pause Workflow 1 on that record.

## Proposals

Custom corporate jobs get a branded PDF plus a short SC@ cover email. Copy the Route three-paragraph shape. Hold every NEW proposal for her explicit yes before sending.

## Quote Follow-Up

Follow-up drafts on day 3 and day 8 only if she says yes. Do not auto-draft them. Reply in the original thread.

## After booking

Sprout agreement and invoice. Google Calendar event with the Sprout link. Sprout W2 Booked Client Sequence does not send a booking confirmation. Existing per-type thank-you workflows already fire on Booked.

Assistants may book, reschedule, and invoice in Sprout. Purchases and email sends always need her yes.

## Shoot Day

Cards to Dropbox `Headshot Sessions`. Never format cards until backed up in two places. Capture One color-tag law lives in [business.md](business.md).

## Post-Shoot Delivery

Evoto then Photoshop. Client-named files. Informal turnaround: studio 24-48 hours; teams 2-3 business days; events 5-7. Proposal PDFs still say 2-3 business days as the formal line. If delivery will miss the window, tell the client before the deadline.

## Review and Referral

Review request about 3 days after the gallery: https://g.page/r/CcstwGsbi8ffEAE/review. One follow-up +7 days if they were happy. Never ask twice more. Skip if the session had friction.

When a review lands, reply on Google within 2 days and name the session type. Referral thank-you same week. Reactivation 18+ months. If a client mentions more people at their company, follow up within 2 days.
