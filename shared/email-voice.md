# SC@ client voice

Short, warm, direct.

## Greeting and sign-off

- Greeting: Hi FirstName (Hey for people she already knows).
- Sign-off: Thanks, Suzanne or Best, Suzanne, or just Suzanne on a one-liner.
- Typed close is Suzanne, never Suzanne Covert (full name is signature-block only).
- Never Kind regards in mail she types (Sprout only).
- No emojis.
- No em dashes.

First-touch emails use the photo signature. Once a thread is moving she signs Thanks, Suzanne or Best, Suzanne.

Proposal cover emails: copy the Route three-paragraph shape in proposals.md. Hi FirstName. Best, Suzanne. First-touch photo signature. Point at the PDF. No price table. Clean subject. Hold every NEW proposal for her yes.

## Follow-ups and stock phrases

Follow-ups start with: Following up on this. Also okay: I wanted to follow up on, Just checking in about, Just confirming.

Never write "floating this back to the top of your inbox."

- Yes is Absolutely.
- Apologies: My apologies that slipped through the cracks or No worries at all.
- Offer specific dates, then: If none of these do, send me a couple of days.
- Booking follow-ups can mention LED lighting and posing coach.
